METHODOLOGY & NOTES
Rules applied to build the season data
Source: 28 original Charter Oak CC tee sheet PDFs (May 2 – August 2, 2026), parsed directly from each PDF's text layer using coordinate-based column detection, last rebuilt August 3, 2026.
RuleApplication
Time WindowOnly tee times from 8:00 AM through 10:50 AM inclusive are included as "regular play," applied uniformly to every date including tournament days.
Tournament DatesJune 27 (Invitational Tournament, course closed all day) and July 11 (Club Championship Weekend) are full-day club tournaments, but their pre-10:50 AM tee times are still counted under the uniform time-window rule above rather than excluded outright.
Checksum ValidationEvery PDF's extracted player count was verified against that PDF's own stated "(N Players)" header count. 27 of 28 dates matched exactly (96.4%); Saturday, July 25 showed 201 players extracted against a stated header count of 203 (2-player gap), unresolved after a cell-by-cell cross-check against the PDF's text layer. Sunday, July 26 initially showed 217 against a stated 221 (4-player gap); a follow-up manual page-by-page visual review against the rendered PDF images (rather than just the extracted text/table data) found the exact cause — two entire table rows (1:10 pm and 1:20 pm) each had their 3rd and 4th player cells silently dropped by the table-extraction step, with no blank-cell placeholder left behind to flag the loss. All 4 players were recovered from the source PDF and Sunday, July 26 now matches its stated count exactly (221/221). Saturday, August 1 initially extracted only 162 of 187 players; 21 cells had their holes/transport detail silently dropped (table extraction cut them off mid-cell) and 4 more were dropped entirely as blank/None cells. All 25 were recovered via cross-reference against the PDF's raw text layer, bringing the date to an exact match (187/187). Sunday, August 2 initially extracted 163 of 188; 23 cells (mostly guest entries) had holes/transport silently dropped and 2 more were blank/None cells. All 25 were recovered the same way, matching exactly (188/188).
Name ReconstructionPDF text extraction sometimes glues two adjacent players' names together or splits a name fragment across a column boundary; both patterns are detected and repaired automatically before counting. On July 26, the table-extraction step additionally dropped one player's cell entirely from a foursome (recovered via the PDF's raw text layer), split another player's category across two table rows (also recovered from the text layer), and — found only via manual visual review, see Checksum Validation — dropped 4 players across two entire rows with no trace in the extracted table structure at all.
Holes InferenceNew for July 26: 22 player entries had their holes/transport detail truncated by the table-extraction step with no recoverable text nearby. Rather than mark all 22 as "Not Determinable," each was inferred to have played the same number of holes as the other players in their own tee time (foursomes overwhelmingly play the same course length together) — all 22 were resolved this way, so none were added to "Not Determinable from Sheet" for July 26.
Guest CategoriesGuest, Family Guest, and Comp are grouped as Guest for category/member classification purposes. As of July 25, a new guest subtype was observed for the first time: "9 Hole Guest (T-Thu)" — also grouped as Guest.
Guest Fee Definition"Guest Fees" counts only fee-paying categories (Guest + Family Guest = 120, regular-play window, as of July 26). Comp (complimentary, 127 rounds) is tracked separately since no fee is charged.
New Membership Category"Governor's Privilege" appeared for the first time on July 25 (1 round, regular-play window). "Out of State" appeared for the first time on July 26 (1 round, entire-day only — outside the 8–10:50 AM window). Neither folds into any existing summary category — both are now tracked as their own rows in the Category Summary tables.
Holes Not Determinable4 guest entries on July 25 (regular-play window) listed only a name and category with no holes/transport/fee info recorded on the source PDF. These are counted in "Not Determinable from Sheet" rather than guessed as 9 or 18 holes. (July 26's otherwise-similar cases were resolved via the Holes Inference rule above instead.)
Category Not Shown on SheetNew for Sunday, August 2: unlike every prior PDF, this report omitted the membership category for most players (only guest-type entries showed one) — only 23 of 188 cells had an explicit category. Categories for the rest were filled in by looking up each player's known category from the existing 353-member season database. 59 players (4 within the 8–10:50 AM regular-play window, 55 outside it) had no prior record and no category shown on the sheet at all; these are tracked as "Not Determinable" — a new row in the Category Summary tables — rather than guessed.
Round DefinitionEach player appearance in a qualifying regular-play tee time counts as one round.
Both Days, Same WeekendEach of the 14 Saturday/Sunday pairs (May 2–3 through August 1–2) is checked individually per member; the Member Round Totals column counts how many of those weekends a member played both days. August 1–2 is now a complete paired weekend, contributing to this update.
WeatherWeekend weather is sourced from the Hudson, MA station via Weather Underground historical data. All season weather gaps have been filled in as of August 7.
Data Quality FixTwo long-standing issues were found and corrected while merging August 2 data: a duplicate member row for "D'Amico, Anthony" (one copy with an HTML-encoded apostrophe, one plain) was merged into a single correct row (5 total rounds), and an unrelated row literally named "Player 3 O'Sullivan, Brendan" (1 round) — an old table-extraction artifact that glued a column header onto a name — was left in place since it wasn't fully clear whether to merge or delete it; flagged here for a follow-up look.
